Nomo Trade Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 18, 2026

Nomo Trade (the "App") is an information-only app that shows US stocks which passed an automated trading bot's checks. This policy explains what information the App handles and what our advertising SDK may collect.

1. Information we collect directly

The App does not require or collect accounts, logins, names, contact details, location, photos or payment information. The operator does not store personal information that could identify App users.

2. Server requests and access logs

  • To load stock picks and market data, the App sends read requests to the operator's data server (Supabase). These requests contain no value that identifies you.
  • As with most hosting providers, ordinary access records such as IP address, request time and device/network information may be kept on the server side. The operator does not use these records to identify individuals or for marketing.

3. Information stored on your device

  • So that the App can show the last known information offline, the most recently received picks, market data, history and parts of charts are stored only on your device.
  • Whether you acknowledged the investment notice (the document version) is also stored on your device.
  • This information is not sent anywhere and is removed when you delete the App.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

  • The App may show Google AdMob banner ads on some screens. Ads are unrelated to the stock picks.
  • To deliver and measure ads, the Google AdMob SDK may collect the advertising identifier (IDFA, if you allow tracking), IP address, device information (such as model and OS version) and ad interaction data.
  • Google processes this information. See Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.
  • If you do not allow tracking, non-personalized ads are shown.

5. Your choices

  • Tracking: You can change whether the App may track you at any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. The App works the same either way.
  • Ad consent: In regions where a consent flow applies (such as the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), you can change your ad privacy settings again from the App's "Info & terms" screen.
  • Deletion: Deleting the App removes all information stored on your device.

6. Third parties and processing abroad

  • The operator does not sell personal information and does not share user information with third parties other than the advertising SDK described in Section 4.
  • The data server (Supabase) and the advertising service (Google) may process information on servers located outside your country.

7. Children

The App is not directed to children under 14 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will post the changes and the effective date on this page.

9. Contact

For privacy-related questions, please open an issue at GitHub Issues. It is a public board, so please do not post personal information.
